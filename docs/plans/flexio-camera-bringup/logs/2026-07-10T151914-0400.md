Strategy checkpoint after visual review of the synthetic-frame pipeline test.

expected from the current ping-pong synthetic pattern, but it is not useful

Move to real FlexIO/eDMA data movement. The camera data lines are currently

movement into RAM is observable and stable with the disconnected/pulled bus.
