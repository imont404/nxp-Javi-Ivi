# Log: signal-instrumentation

Built and flashed a guarded VSYNC interrupt diagnostic for the Port 4 camera
sync wiring. The firmware now counts `P4_22` VSYNC rising edges with
`GPIO40_IRQn`, while keeping a guard that disables the VSYNC interrupt and
falls back to flag polling if more than 120 VSYNC edges are seen in one second.

Commands:

```powershell
.\build_cmake.ps1
.\flash.ps1 -CMake
.\rtt.ps1 -CMake -Reset -Seconds 12
```

Build and flash completed successfully. RTT showed the cleaned/terminated
VSYNC line now behaves plausibly as a frame-rate signal:

- `P4_21` HSYNC/HREF remained stable at about 6036 to 6042 rising edges/sec.
- `P4_22` VSYNC counted about 30 to 31 rising edges/sec after the initial
  startup interval.
- `p4_lines=200`, matching the expected relationship of about 6040 HSYNC
  edges/sec divided by about 30 VSYNC edges/sec.
- `P4_20` PCLK continued to show a polled edge flag each interval.
- `p4_vs_off=0`, so the VSYNC IRQ guard did not trip.
- The original/reference Port 0 camera pins remained static.
