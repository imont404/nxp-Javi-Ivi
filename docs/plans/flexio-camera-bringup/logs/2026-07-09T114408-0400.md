# Log: signal-instrumentation

Completed the VSYNC wiring cleanup. The new Port 4 camera signal jumpers were
shortened where practical, and the VSYNC line to `P4_22` now has the same
330 ohm pulldown/termination treatment as the other camera signal lines.

Current diagnostic mapping remains:

- Camera VSYNC to `P4_22`.

changes, then decide whether to re-enable `P4_22` VSYNC as a GPIO interrupt
