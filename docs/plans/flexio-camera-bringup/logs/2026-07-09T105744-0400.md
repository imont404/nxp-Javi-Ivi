# Log: signal-instrumentation

Completed the first FlexIO diagnostic wiring pass:

- Camera PCLK to `P4_20`.
- Camera HSYNC/HREF to `P4_21`.
- Camera VSYNC to `P4_22`.
- Existing SCCB/I2C, XCLK, reset, power-down, and data wiring remain unchanged.

The three new signal jumpers are longer than the existing camera wiring, about
6 inches total. The existing lines have low-value pulldown termination for noise
control, but the new PCLK/HSYNC/VSYNC jumpers do not yet have that added.
Signals were checked at the board side and PCLK, HSYNC/HREF, and VSYNC are being
generated and reaching the jumpers. Treat early diagnostics cautiously if edge
counts look unstable.

Next firmware step is deliberately slow: start with interrupt-driven HSYNC/HREF
and VSYNC counts, use PCLK as a sampled/debug signal first, and add stronger
PCLK measurement only after the sync counts look plausible.
