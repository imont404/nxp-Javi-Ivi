# Log: port1-signal-diag

Flashed `avc_core0-FlexIOPort1` and captured RTT. **Partial success: sync path
works, data path does not.**

J-Link pinned to 1066857635 throughout; the other probe on this machine
(851003830) was not touched.

## Working

```
FlexIO camera eDMA pins: D0-D7=P1_4..P1_11/FLEXIO0_D12..D19 PCLK=P1_14/FLEXIO0_D22 HREF=P0_11/FLEXIO0_D3 VSYNC=P0_4/GPIO IRQ
FlexIO camera pin mux readback: data(D7..D0)=0x66666666 pclk=6 href=6 vsync=0 OK
```

- **Every pin muxed as intended.** The refactor is correct; this is not a
  firmware pin-setup problem.
- **VSYNC fires at the right rate.** `vs_us=42757/42752/42798`, i.e. 42.76 ms =
  **23.4 FPS**, matching the documented EZH baseline of 23.43 FPS. The jumpered
  frame-start path on `P0_4` through `GPIO00_IRQHandler` works, and the
  promotion of that handler from reference counter to capture trigger is sound.
- eDMA arms every frame: `start=` advances steadily.

## Not working

```
cam_dma fps=0.00 done=0(+0) start=49(+6) busy_vs=146(+17) timeout=48(+5)
        sample_nz=64 first=0000,0000,0000,0000 rem=3438 shiftstat=00 shifterr=00
cam_dma ...                                    rem=0    shiftstat=FF shifterr=FF
```

- **No frame ever completes.** `done` stays 0.
- The eDMA major loop is 4000 minor loops of 32 bytes. `rem` sits around
  3400-4000, so only roughly 100-600 minor loops occur per frame instead of
  4000. Data is being clocked, but far too little of it.
- **All eight data bits read zero** across every sample.
- When a transfer does drain to `rem=0`, `shiftstat=FF shifterr=FF` - error on
  all eight shifters.

## Ruled out

- **Pin muxing.** Readback says OK.
- **A PCLK/HREF adjacency assumption.** `camera__configure_flexio_camera()` is
  fully parameterised: `TRGSEL = 2 * CAMERA_FLEXIO_HREF_PIN` and
  `PINSEL = CAMERA_FLEXIO_PCLK_PIN`. Port 4 happened to have PCLK and HREF
  adjacent (D28/D29) and Port 1 does not (D22/D3), but nothing depends on that.
- **The EZH pin setup clobbering PORT1.** `camera__configure_ezh_pins()` is not
  called in this build, and the mux readback post-dates any such write.
- **Display init clobbering the data pins.** Nothing outside the EZH path
  writes `PORT1->PCR[4..11]`.

## The open question

Sync timing arrives correctly while data reads all zeros. That combination says
the camera and its VSYNC/HREF are alive, but the eight data lines are either not
reaching `FLEXIO0_D12..D19` or are not being sampled at the right instant.

Candidates, untested:

1. The three jumper stubs degrade PCLK enough to break sampling while leaving
   VSYNC (a slow signal) intact. Plausible: PCLK is the fastest signal on the
   link and now has a dupont stub.
2. HREF on `FLEXIO0_D3` behaves differently as a timer trigger than
   `FLEXIO0_D29` did. Nothing in the reference manual reading so far suggests a
   lower/upper-half restriction, but it has not been ruled out.
3. Camera data is genuinely not present on `P1_4..P1_11` for a reason not
   visible from firmware.

## Recommended next diagnostic

Sample `FLEXIO0->PIN` - the read-only live state of all FlexIO pins - in the
`cam_dma` report. That directly distinguishes "data is not arriving on
D12..D19" from "data arrives but is sampled wrong", which is exactly the fork
above and cannot be resolved by reasoning from the current counters.

A scope on `P1_14` versus `P0_5` would settle candidate 1 immediately.
