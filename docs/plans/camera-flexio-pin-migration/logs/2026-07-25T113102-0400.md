# Log: port1-frame-capture

**Port 1 FlexIO capture works.** The premise of this plan is proven: three
jumpers convert the Rev A camera wiring to FlexIO, and the EZH is no longer
needed for capture.

## Root cause of the earlier failure

Operator wiring slip — the PCLK jumper was one slot over on `J9_EXT`. Refitted
correctly and capture came up immediately. Nothing in firmware was wrong.

Worth noting for the record: the previous log named PCLK as the leading
suspect, and the pin-mux readback correctly reported `OK` throughout, which is
exactly what it is for. The readback said "firmware muxed what it intended", so
attention went to the wiring rather than to the refactor. That is the diagnostic
split it was added to provide.

        sample_nz=64 first=2143,31C4,31E4,31E4

| Measure | Port 1 FlexIO | EZH baseline |
|---|---|---|
| Frame rate | **23.39 fps** | 23.43 fps |
| Frame time | 42.42 ms | ~42.7 ms |
| VSYNC period | 42.76 ms | 42.76 ms |

- **`done` tracks `start`** with a difference of one, which is the in-flight
  transfer. Every frame completes.
- **Zero errors**: `timeout`, `busy_vs`, `submit_err`, `cb_err` all flat at 0,
  and `shiftstat`/`shifterr` clean.
- **Real pixel data**: `first=2143,31C4,31E4,31E4` and `sample_nz=64`, versus
  all-zero samples before the fix.
- Image confirmed live on the LCD by the operator.
- `p4_vs=0` as expected; the Port 4 VSYNC counter is unused in this group.

Frame rate matches EZH, which is the success bar — this plan was never about
throughput.

## Note for Rev B

This was achieved with dupont stubs on three signals, and it still hit the EZH
frame rate with zero shifter errors. A properly routed Rev B should be at least
as good. Do not read a PCLK ceiling out of this setup either way.
