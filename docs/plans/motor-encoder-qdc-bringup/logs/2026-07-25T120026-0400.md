# Log: dual-qdc-count-diag

**Channel independence proven.** Drove M0 at 10% and M1 at 20% PWM
simultaneously.

| | mean counts/sec | sd | vs both-at-10% baseline |
|---|---|---|---|
| M0 @ 10% | 680.9 | 2.33 | 677.9 -> **+0.4%** |
| M1 @ 20% | 1450.1 | 57.98 | 739.4 -> **+96.1%** |

Rate ratio M1/M0 = **2.130** against a PWM ratio of 2.000.

**Why this test matters:** driving both motors at one duty cannot distinguish a
correctly wired pair from a cross-wired one, or from two channels accidentally
reading the same source. Here M0 stayed put to within 0.4% while M1 nearly
exactly doubled. No cross-wiring or duplicate-read fault survives that. QDC0 is
M0 (J17) and QDC1 is M1 (J11), independently and correctly.

Two secondary observations:

- **PWM to speed is roughly linear in this range**, slightly super-linear
  (2.13x speed for 2x duty), which is the expected shape as fixed friction
  losses matter less at higher duty.
- **M1's variance rises sharply at 20%** — sd 57.98 versus 2.33 for M0 at 10%.
  Some of that is genuine speed ripple, but on blocks it may also be
  vibration. Worth watching when PID tuning; the noise floor is not constant
  across the speed range.

## Auto-start added

A scripted RTT capture window and a human pressing the center button race each
other, and the capture kept winning — two runs were lost to it.

`CONFIG__MOTOR_ENCODER_DIAG_AUTOSTART_MS` (0 = button only, the default) starts
the motors a fixed delay after the diagnostic loop begins.
`.\build_motor_encoder_diag.ps1 -AutoStartMs 4000`. Still gated behind
`-EnableMotors` and the 20% duty cap, the button still toggles afterwards, and
the banner says the car must be on blocks.

This made the measurement repeatable, which is what let the comparison above be
made against the earlier baseline at all.

## Per-motor PWM added

`CONFIG__MOTOR_ENCODER_DIAG_PWM_PERCENT_M0` / `_M1`, both defaulting to the
shared `CONFIG__MOTOR_ENCODER_DIAG_PWM_PERCENT` so existing behaviour is
unchanged. Exposed as `-PwmPercentM0` / `-PwmPercentM1`. Both are individually
range-checked against the same 0-20 cap.
