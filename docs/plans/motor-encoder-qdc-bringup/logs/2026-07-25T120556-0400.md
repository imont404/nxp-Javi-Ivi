# Log: wheel-geometry-calibration

**Counts per wheel revolution measured: 1320.** The vendor contradiction is
settled.

## Measurement

Motors-off image, position zeroed at reset. Operator marked a wheel and turned
it exactly 10 revolutions by hand.

```
m0: final_pos=13188  net_delta=13188  moving_samples=103
m1: final_pos=0      net_delta=0
```

**13188 counts / 10 revolutions = 1318.8 per revolution**, which is 1320 to
within **0.09 percent**. The 12-count shortfall is not landing exactly back on
the mark, exactly what a hand spin should look like. M1 read exactly zero
throughout, confirming again that the channels are independent.

This matches `gear_ratio 30 x 11 encoder lines x 4 (quadrature) = 1320`.

## Which vendor figure was right

| Source | Gear ratio | Implied counts/rev | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hiwonder spec table | 1:90 | 3960 | **wrong** |
| Hiwonder support answer | 1:30 | **1320** | **correct** |

The customer measurement quoted in the product-page Q&A (1320 over ten hand
rotations) was right, and the spec table on the same page is wrong.

## Correcting an inference I recorded earlier

The previous log leaned toward 3960 on a rated-speed argument: the motor is
rated 110 rpm no-load, so 10 percent duty ought to give roughly 11 rpm, which
fit 3960 and not 1320.

**That reasoning was wrong, and it is worth knowing why.** At 10 percent duty
the wheel actually turns about 31 rpm - roughly 28 percent of no-load speed, not
10 percent. **PWM duty is strongly non-linear in speed at the low end**, with a
large offset. Do not estimate wheel speed from duty; measure it. This is also a
useful thing to show students who assume duty maps to speed.

The earlier log did flag the argument as suggestive rather than conclusive, and
the measurement is why it did not become a wrong constant in the firmware.

## Applied

`CONFIG__MOTOR_ENCODER_COUNTS_PER_WHEEL_REV` default is now **1320**, with the
measurement and the non-linearity warning recorded at the definition.

Added `CONFIG__MOTOR_ENCODER_WHEEL_DIAMETER_MM` = **75**, matching the chassis
ground-to-top-plate height, for converting rate to ground speed. Wheel
circumference is 235.6 mm.

**Bug found while verifying:** `build_motor_encoder_diag.ps1` passed
`-CountsPerWheelRev` with a default of 0 as a `-Define`, which overrode the
header and forced rpm to read zero regardless of the header value. The wrapper
now omits the define unless explicitly given, so the measured constant wins.
